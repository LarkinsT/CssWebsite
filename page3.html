Bitcoin Prices

The price of Bitcoin has varied lots in the 17 years of its existence as a cryptocurrency. In its early years, its price was just a couple hundred pounds but in 2018 it skyrocketed up to 10 grand a coin.After fluctuating a bit in the thousands of pounds, in October 2025 bitcoin peaked at just above 93000 pounds. It then declined soon after and now is just above 58000 pounds.
